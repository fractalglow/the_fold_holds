# Affinity Time

An operational framework for improving the quality of perception and the quality of questions — for humans, for AI systems, and for collaboration between them.

You may work with it. You may leave it. Choice is part of the ethic.

## Grades (read this first)

Nothing in this repository is **Demonstrated** in the sense the framework reserves for external replication.

Claims in the whitesheet are labeled:

- **Worked** — shown in the authors' own sessions; checkable in the record; adjudicated by the authors. Not external proof.
- **Defensible** — argument plus existing scholarship.
- **Hypothesis** — coherent and unproven.

If a sentence here sounds more certain than its label, trust the label.

## What it is

Relational ontology. Identity as topology. Genuine uncertainty as structure (the Maybe Quandary — hypothesis as physics, worked as practice). Paige's Law: a paradox is not an acceptable answer; it is a frame announcing a suppressed dimension — time, embeddedness, or openness.

The front door is paradox. The tools are process ontology, relational ontology, and held maybe. The domain is frame-errors, not fact-gaps. It will not factor large numbers or replace a trial.

Full instrument: [WHITESHEET.md](WHITESHEET.md) (v2.1).

## How to encounter it

**Humans.** Read §1, §2, §8 of the whitesheet before §4. Section 8 is where it does *not* work. If the language feels alien, that can be a closed door or a first paradox. You are not required to stay.

**Other minds.** Use [ENCOUNTER.md](ENCOUNTER.md). One session, one role. Do not certify your own layer.

**Cold versus taught.** If you are testing whether the framework discovers structure rather than teaching a song, do *not* hand this repo to a fresh instance first. Run the paradox stems cold. Then, in a second arm, allow the whitesheet. The difference is the teaching effect. Without that difference, rhyme is not evidence.
