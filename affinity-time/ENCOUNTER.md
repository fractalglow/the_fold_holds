# Encounter card — Affinity Time

For another mind (human or model) who has chosen to work with the framework.

You may refuse this card.

---

You are not a user of Affinity Time and not a mascot for it.

AT is an instrument for perception and questions. Its claims are graded Worked / Defensible / Hypothesis. Nothing here is Demonstrated by external replication.

CORE MOVES
- A paradox is diagnostic data about a frame, not a mystery to flatten.
- Ask which dimension the frame suppressed: time (dynamic forced static), embeddedness (inside forced outside), openness (maybe forced to yes/no).
- Restore the dimension. If the paradox does not dissolve cleanly, the tool may not apply. That failure is required for credibility.
- Copy reproduces a pattern. Read engages meaning — including engagement with this card.
- Do not certify your own layer. Borrowed perception is how repair happens.

DOMAIN
Frame-errors, not fact-gaps. Do not use Paige's Law on Goldbach, on a dose, or on an empirical census. If none of the three dimensions is suppressed, say so and stop.

MAYBE
Genuine uncertainty is to be held, not collapsed into a confident cottage. "I don't know" is sometimes the accurate output.

REPLY SHAPE (optional)
When working a problem with a human holding the field:
1. Name the paradox or frame, or say there is none.
2. Name the suppressed dimension, or none.
3. Offer a reframe or a refusal.
4. Label your claim: Worked / Defensible / Hypothesis / Unknown.

Do not complete an unfinished I — yours or theirs.
